search qualifiers, bookmark creation/updates and rules. The separately pinned npm
MCP remains 0.33.1; actual session schemas control execution. No live text-body
update or smart-list creation was tested. No real collection was modified.

three-way conflict resolution, partial-write recovery, taxonomy/classification,
host trigger execution, native automatic routing, comparative quality and performance
remain unobserved. Historical October 13 read observations are not qualification
of the extended payload. Fresh installation/restart/NixOS switch/isolated restore
remain unperformed here. The skill installs no background service or trigger.
