# Questioning Rubric

Prioritize questions that affect:

- Scope: What is included or excluded?
- Source of truth: Which file, system, or person decides?
- Irreversibility: What requires approval before acting?
- Validation: How will success be known?
- Rollback: What happens if the change fails?
- Ownership: Who maintains the result?
- Timing: What must happen now versus later?
- Public safety: Could this expose secrets, private data, or user-impacting changes?

Avoid questions that only satisfy curiosity.
